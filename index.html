About this website

Please read before “buying”

  • Shoppi is not a real store. It is a shopping simulation built for fun and demonstration. No real products are sold, and nothing will ever be shipped.
  • No real payments are processed. The PayPal-style checkout is entirely simulated — no money moves, no accounts are accessed, and no transaction happens anywhere.
  • Any card number works. Card numbers, expiry dates, and security codes you enter are never validated, stored, or sent anywhere.
  • Nothing leaves your browser. There is no server, no account, and no data collection. Everything you do happens locally on this page.
  • Your data stays local. Your cart, wishlist, coupons, and settings are saved only in your own browser using localStorage.
  • All prices, ratings, and reviews are fictional and generated automatically for each product.
  • The gear icon opens Settings, where you can configure purchase limits, verification steps, and hold-to-confirm for large purchases.
